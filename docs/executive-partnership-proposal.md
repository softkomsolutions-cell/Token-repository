## 1. The opportunity and product vision

Real-world asset (RWA) tokenisation is redefining asset management globally.
This platform bridges high-value physical assets, including prime real estate
and high-yield collectibles such as luxury shoes, Lego sets, and art, with
modern fractional investment.

By utilizing advanced blockchain infrastructure and web automation, the
platform reduces standard investment friction. The goal is to allow everyday
investors to acquire fractional, income-generating ownership shares in seconds.

### Core SaaS modules

- **Investor Portal:** A premium, banking-grade web dashboard where users track
  portfolio growth, monitor live secondary market values, and collect automated
  dividend payouts.
- **Asset Originator Portal:** A secure backend system for asset managers to
  ingest physical assets, log legal title or appraisal certificates, and manage
  supply distribution.
- **Unified AI Co-Pilot:** A natural-language conversational AI layer over the
  platform that provides plain-English market data analytics and asset
  valuations for investors.

## 2. System architecture and compliance blueprint

Building an RWA platform is a specialized enterprise undertaking. The
architecture requires high-level data security and strict regulatory compliance.
The proposed platform uses a scalable cloud-to-blockchain hybrid stack:

```text
[Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind] --> [User Onboarding: Privy embedded wallets]
                                                |
                                                v
[Relational Engine: Supabase] --> [Security Layer: Sumsub automated KYC webhooks]
                                                |
                                                v
                                 [Blockchain Protocols: ERC-3643 / ERC-1155]
```

- **Frictionless Web3 webhooks:** Privy or Dynamic.xyz can allow users to sign
  up using email or Google accounts while the platform creates a secure
  blockchain wallet in the background.
- **Asset fractionalisation:** Audited Solidity ERC-3643 and ERC-1155 tokens can
  enforce compliance directly on-chain, blocking token transfers when a buyer
  has not passed identity verification.
- **Automated regulatory KYC:** Identity verification integrations through
  secure webhooks, such as Sumsub or Persona, support anti-money laundering
  (AML) compliance workflows.

## 5. Revenue pipeline and SaaS monetisation

The platform is designed around multiple recurring revenue streams:

1. **Asset originator setup fees:** R50,000 to R100,000 flat implementation fees
   per asset pool to cover legal, audit, and onboarding overhead.
2. **Platform subscription fees:** R5,000 to R10,000 monthly active SaaS fees
   charged to asset management entities to keep investor terminals active.
3. **Assets under management (AUM) fee:** A continuous 0.5% to 2.0% annualized
   asset management fee drawn from underlying rental or flip yields.
